Start here

This should feel simple.

The last screen will not dump everything back at you. It will give you a plain-language read on your plan, the main gap, the best next move, and what to watch.

A strategy is not “whatever I feel like doing after a bad meeting.”

A strategy connects your goal, your options, your resources, and your risk.

You will leave with
  • A simple read on your plan
  • The first thing to do next
  • The biggest gap to build
  • A short watch-out list
